How fast can Zailux be set up for my business? +

Our onboarding team trains and deploys your custom Zailux AI Receptionist within 96 hours of receiving your business information, FAQs, and booking link.

Does Zailux integrate with my calendar & CRM? +

Yes! Zailux integrates seamlessly with Google Calendar, Outlook, Calendly, GoHighLevel, HubSpot, Zapier, and major custom CRM systems.

What happens if Zailux can't answer a customer question? +

If a caller asks something outside Zailux's knowledge scope, the AI politely offers to forward the call directly to your cell phone or records a detailed voice message and texts/emails you immediately.

Can I customize the voice and accent of the receptionist? +

Absolutely. You can select male or female voices, regional accents (US, UK, Australian, etc.), and adjust the pitch, tone, and pacing to match your brand's unique identity.
