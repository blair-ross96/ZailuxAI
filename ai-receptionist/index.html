Frequently asked questions

Good to know before you get started.

Here are answers to common questions about using an AI receptionist with your business.

Will customers know they're speaking to AI?

Zailux can be transparent that it's an AI assistant, or you can choose how it introduces itself to callers. Either way, Zailux is designed to communicate naturally, professionally and in a way that reflects your brand. Some people can't tell they are speaking to AI!

Can it book directly into my calendar?

Yes. Zailux can connect with your calendar and book appointments based on your availability. It can also help with cancellations, rescheduling and appointment confirmations. Integrations include Google, Calendly and cal.com etc.

Can Zailux transfer calls to my team?

Yes. Zailux can identify when a caller needs to speak with a member of your team and transfer the call to the appropriate person or department.

What happens if Zailux can't answer a question?

If Zailux encounters a question it hasn't been trained on, it can politely take a message, transfer the call to a human team member, or follow up via email/SMS.

How quickly can I get started?

Most businesses are fully set up and running within 5 business days. We handle the entire build, testing, and integration for you.

What is included in the free trial?

The trial lets you experience your configured service for 7 days before the monthly subscription begins. We'll confirm the activation date, included usage and any applicable call charges before you start.

Can I keep my existing business phone number?

In many cases, there may be a way to keep using the number your customers already know, but the right option depends on your current provider, country and number type. Options may include porting an eligible number into the phone system or forwarding calls from your existing line to the number configured for Zailux. We'll review your setup during onboarding and confirm the practical options before making changes. Do not cancel your existing phone service until any transfer or forwarding arrangement has been tested and confirmed.

Can I choose when Zailux answers calls?

Yes. We can configure Zailux to answer around the clock, only outside your normal business hours, or as a backup when your existing phone rings out. The final behaviour depends on the agreed call-routing setup and the phone service used.

Can Zailux recognise existing customers?

Yes, this can be enabled as an option. When a caller uses a phone number already associated with a contact in the system, Zailux can use the stored contact information, such as their name, to personalise the greeting. Your business can choose whether to use this feature.

Are call logs, transcripts and summaries available?

Call logs and transcripts are available. AI-generated call summaries and post-call workflows can also be configured to help your team review what happened and decide what to do next.

Can Zailux send booking confirmations and reminders?

Yes, booking confirmations and appointment reminders can be configured using supported messaging and workflow options. Automatic texts after a missed call are handled separately through the optional Missed Call Text Back add-on.

What happens when I use all 2,000 minutes?

Any call usage above the 2,000 minutes included in your monthly plan is invoiced separately. If you expect to use more minutes regularly, contact enquire@zailux.ai to discuss a higher-volume arrangement.

What happens during the seven-day free trial?

We first discuss your requirements, collect the relevant business information, build your receptionist and test the agreed call flow. Your seven-day trial only begins when the configured system is ready and goes live, so you can trial the setup built for your business rather than an unfinished configuration.

Can I cancel?

Once you cancel your subscription, you can continue using Zailux until the end of your current billing month. After that, your AI agents will be taken offline. Your contacts and data will remain in the CRM for one month, giving you time to export and take your data with you before it is removed from the Zailux platform.
