Frequently asked questions

Good to know before you get started.

Here are answers to common questions about using an AI receptionist with your business.

Will callers know they're speaking to AI?

The receptionist uses an AI voice to handle the conversation. You can decide how it introduces itself, and any disclosure should follow the requirements that apply to your business and location.

Can it book appointments into my calendar?

It can support appointment booking when a compatible calendar and booking workflow are connected and configured during setup. We'll confirm the exact workflow for your business.

Can it transfer a call to a person?

Call transfers can be configured where supported by the phone setup. We'll agree which situations should be transferred and what should happen if nobody is available.

What happens if it doesn't know an answer?

We can configure it to avoid guessing, explain that your team will need to help, capture the caller's details and follow the agreed escalation or callback process.

How long does setup take?

Our target setup timeline is within 7 days, depending on the information, integrations and call flows required. We'll confirm your timeline during onboarding.

What is included in the free trial?

The trial lets you experience your configured service for 7 days before the monthly subscription begins. We'll confirm the activation date, included usage and any applicable call charges before you start.

What happens after 2,000 minutes?

The plan includes 2,000 inbound call minutes per month. Additional usage is charged at A$0.45 per minute.

Can I cancel?

Zailux subscriptions are intended to be month-to-month. Contact the Zailux team for the cancellation process and timing applicable to your account.
