How it works

From first contact to booked appointment.

Whichever channel is used, the AI Sales Rep follows a consistent process to help turn suitable leads into real sales opportunities.

01 — IDENTIFY

A lead is identified

A new enquiry arrives, an existing warm lead needs follow-up or an eligible missed opportunity is identified.

02 — CONTACT

Your AI makes contact

The configured workflow initiates an appropriate voice call or messaging conversation.

03 — QUALIFY

The lead is qualified

The AI asks relevant questions, gathers information and checks whether the prospect is ready for the next step.

04 — BOOK

The next step is arranged

The AI helps book an appointment where configured, and records the prospect’s answers to the qualification questions. Your salesperson receives useful notes on the lead’s needs and what was discussed, so they can go into the call prepared.

The handover

Your team gets the conversation context, not just a booking.

When the AI captures qualification answers, it can pass the key details into the configured workflow so your salesperson has a useful starting point before the appointment.

  • The prospect’s needs and main reason for enquiring
  • Answers to the qualification questions you agreed
  • Relevant details, concerns and preferred next step
  • Appointment information where booking is configured

Frequently asked questions

Good to know before you get started.

Learn how the AI Sales Rep can help move leads towards booked appointments.

What is an AI Sales Rep?

An AI Sales Rep helps your business contact leads, qualify prospects, answer approved common enquiries and move suitable opportunities towards an appointment or human sales conversation.

Does the AI Sales Rep only make phone calls?

No. Voice calls and messaging are different communication channels serving the same goal: contact leads, qualify them and help book appointments. Supported messaging channels may include SMS, WhatsApp, Facebook Messenger, Instagram Direct Messages and website chat, depending on configuration.

Can it follow up with existing leads?

Yes. Suitable existing leads and previous enquiries can be followed up through configured workflows, subject to contact permissions, channel rules and applicable requirements.

Can it qualify leads before booking an appointment?

Yes. The AI can ask agreed qualifying questions, collect information and help identify whether a prospect is ready for the next step.

Can it book appointments automatically?

It can help arrange appointments when the necessary calendar integration, permissions and booking configuration are in place.

Can it answer questions about my business?

It can answer common questions using the business information and instructions provided during setup. Complex, uncertain or sensitive questions should be escalated appropriately.

Will customers know they're speaking to AI?

The configured agent should be presented honestly and in accordance with applicable disclosure requirements. It should not impersonate a human.

How quickly can I get started?

We aim to have your Zailux AI live within the existing setup timeframe, once your business information and requirements are confirmed and the required integrations are ready.

Can I hear the AI Sales Rep before signing up?

Yes, once the voice-demo integration is configured. The form above will clearly indicate if the call feature is not yet available, rather than pretending a call has been placed.

Are all messaging channels included?

Not necessarily. Channel availability depends on the selected setup, supported integrations, permissions and any applicable usage or provider charges.

Can I try a text conversation before signing up?

Yes, once the SMS-demo integration is configured. The SMS demo form near the top of the page will clearly show whether the messaging service is available, and it will not claim a message was sent unless the backend confirms the request was accepted.
