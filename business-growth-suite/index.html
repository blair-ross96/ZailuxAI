Business Growth Suite FAQs

A few practical answers to help you decide whether the package fits your business.

What is the Business Growth Suite?

It is a package combining access to AI Employee Unlimited, Marketing Tools, Performance Reports and Reputation Management Tools. The toolsets can be useful individually and may complement one another when configured for your business.

Can I buy the tools individually?

Yes. The individual toolsets remain available through the Tools & Add-ons page. The package brings the four listed components together at a combined price.

Does the package include AI Receptionist or AI Sales Rep?

No. AI Receptionist and AI Sales Rep are separate services with their own subscriptions and call-minute allowances.

Does Zailux manage my marketing campaigns?

No. Marketing Tools provide access to the software features. Campaign planning, content creation or publishing, active advertising management and advertising spend are not included unless separately agreed as a service.

Does Zailux manage my reviews for me?

The package provides access to reputation tools for supported review requests, monitoring, response options and widgets. It does not automatically mean Zailux manages reviews or replies to customers on your behalf.

Can I connect my existing accounts?

Some features support connections to third-party accounts. Availability and supported actions vary by platform, account permissions, configuration and each platform's policies.

Will the reports show results immediately?

Reports reflect activity and data that have been recorded. New or inactive accounts, selected date ranges, missing tracking or unconnected advertising accounts may show little or no data.

Do all four toolsets automatically work together?

Not necessarily. They can complement one another, but data connections, integrations and workflows may need to be configured. The pathway on this page is an example of how a business could use them together.

Are advertising costs included?

No. Advertising spend is separate. The package can provide access to supported advertising tools and reporting, but it does not include the money paid to advertising platforms or managed advertising services.

Are the screenshots on this page real?

No. The current gallery contains clearly labelled illustrative placeholders. They are designed to be replaced with genuine product screenshots when available.
