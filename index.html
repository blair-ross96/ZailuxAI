Questions? We have Answers

Will customers know they're speaking to AI? +
Zailux can introduce itself as an AI assistant and use a conversational, professional style that reflects your brand. We’ll agree how it introduces itself and the guidance it follows during setup.
Can Zailux transfer calls to my team? +
Yes. Zailux can identify when a caller needs to speak with a member of your team and transfer the call to the appropriate person or department.
Do we need to change our phone number? +
In most supported setups, you can keep your existing business number and forward calls to your Zailux AI receptionist. We’ll confirm the best call-routing setup for your phone service before launch.
Can it book directly into my calendar? +
Yes. With a supported calendar integration, Zailux can book appointments based on your availability. Booking confirmations and reminders can also be configured as part of your setup. Available options depend on your requirements and supported integrations.
Can it use our existing business information? +
We configure Zailux using the business information you provide, such as your services, FAQs, policies, pricing guidance and operating procedures. We also agree what it should do when it doesn’t have a reliable answer.
Can I choose how Zailux speaks to customers? +
You can choose from supported voice options and configure the speaking style and tone to suit your brand. Available voices, accents and settings depend on the voice options supported for your setup.
Can it speak multiple languages? +
Speak to customers in multiple languages, with a range of voices and accents to suit your business.
Can it follow up with leads automatically? +
The AI Sales Rep can follow up with eligible leads, ask your chosen qualifying questions and help book appointments for your team. Call and messaging workflows are configured around your sales process and the permissions and integrations required.
Can it qualify leads before booking appointments? +
Yes. You choose the qualifying questions, and the AI Sales Rep can collect the answers before helping arrange an appointment. Your team decides the criteria and what should happen next.
What if I only want to use Zailux some of the time? +
You choose when Zailux handles your calls. For example, it can be set up to answer outside your usual business hours, or to step in when your phone rings out and your team can't answer. The exact setup depends on your phone system and call-forwarding options.
How quickly can I get started? +
Most systems can be built, tested and ready to go live within 5 business days, depending on the information and integrations required. Your 7-day free trial starts once your system is configured, tested and live.
How much does Zailux cost? +
Plans start from A$599/month with a 7-day free trial. There is no subscription or setup-fee payment during the trial. If you choose to continue, your first monthly fee and one-time setup fee are charged after the trial.
What happens if Zailux can't answer a question? +
Zailux follows the fallback instructions agreed during setup. Depending on your configuration, it can take a message, transfer the caller to a team member or arrange an appropriate follow-up.
Can I control what Zailux says to my customers? +
Yes, you have complete control over scripts, guidelines, fallback responses, and business knowledge that your AI uses.
